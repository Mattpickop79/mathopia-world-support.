Privacy Policy

Mathopia World is a maths puzzle game for children and young people. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app. Last updated: 19 November 2026.

The short version

Mathopia World does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

The app saves game progress on your device only. This includes the player name and avatar chosen in the game, stars, coins, badges, settings and which worlds are unlocked. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time with Reset all progress in the game, or by deleting the app.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. Before any purchase, the app shows a grown-ups-only check so that children cannot buy things by accident. For more information, see Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children’s privacy

Because the app does not collect personal information, no information about children is collected, stored or shared. The app has no chat, no social features and no links to other websites.

Contacting support

If you email us at mattpickop@icloud.com, we will receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and do not share it. If you open an issue on our public GitHub support page, what you write there is visible to anyone, so please don’t include personal information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email mattpickop@icloud.com.
