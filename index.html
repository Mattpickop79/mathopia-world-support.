Common questions

The game isn’t loading

Close and reopen the app. Make sure your device is running a supported version of iOS or iPadOS. If the problem continues, contact support and include your device model and iOS version.

My progress is missing

Progress may be stored on the device where you played. Check that you are using the same device and have not cleared the app’s website or browser data.

How do I report a problem?

Use the Contact support button above. Describe what happened and the steps to reproduce it. Please don’t include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information. If a child is using the app, a parent or guardian should contact support.

Mathopia World is a maths puzzle game. Support requests submitted through GitHub Issues may be publicly visible; do not include personal or sensitive information.
